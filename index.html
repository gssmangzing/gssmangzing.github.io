    About our school

    Government Secondary School, Mangzing was established in 1975 with the aim of providing quality education and shaping young minds. Since its beginning, the school has been committed to creating a positive and supportive learning environment. The school offers education from Nursery to Class X, focusing on academic excellence, discipline, creativity, and overall student development. We take pride in our dedicated teachers, student achievements, modern learning facilities, and strong values. Our mission is to prepare students with the knowledge, skills, and confidence needed for a bright future.
